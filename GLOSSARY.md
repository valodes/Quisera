# Glossary

- **Recipe**: A household-shared preparation record in Quisera, whether created at home or copied from an identified external source.
- **House recipe**: A recipe created and maintained in Quisera. It is editable by either household user.
- **Imported recipe**: A read-only copy linked to its verified source identifier and synchronization metadata.
- **Recipe metric**: A nutrition or environmental value with optional unit, provenance, and calculation method. Missing values remain unavailable; they are never inferred.
- **Favorite**: A recipe bookmark belonging to one authenticated user, not shared with the other household user.
- **Delivery cycle**: The 14 chronological meal slots from dinner on a verified Quitoque delivery date through lunch seven civil days later.
- **Meal slot**: One civil date and one meal period (`lunch` or `dinner`); an unplanned slot is represented by no planned meal.
- **Planned meal**: A household-shared slot assignment for a selected Quitoque recipe, house recipe, or restaurant entry.
- **Quitoque selection**: A recipe occurrence returned by a specific order's items, retained as a delivery snapshot and never imported as a house recipe.
- **Meal origin**: Whether a slot was assigned by the automatic chronological distribution or chosen manually. Manual assignments are preserved by redistribution.
